# Contributing

- One entry per PR, alphabetical within its section.
- Format: `[name](url) - Why it matters for graph engineering (one line).`
- Must be topology-relevant: patterns, orchestration frameworks, handoff protocols, state/durability, observability, benchmarks, or production case studies for systems with more than one agent or node.
- Single-agent loops, general AI tooling, and classical graph databases/GNNs belong in other lists, not here.
- No marketing pages, no dead links, no self-promotion without substance (working code or a real writeup).
- New sections need 3+ entries to justify themselves.
